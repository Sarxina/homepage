ChatSaga

Your Twitch chat as an RPG town on your stream.

Setting up

  1. Step 1

    Install the Sarxina OBS Plugin Manager

    Close OBS, download the manager from its latest release and unzip it into your OBS folder.

    Then open OBS and tick it in the Docks menu.

  2. Step 2

    Install ChatSaga

    In the manager’s dock, open Choose plugins and press Install next to ChatSaga. Then restart OBS.

  3. Step 3

    Connect Twitch

    Press Connect next to Twitch. On twitch.tv/activate, enter the code shown in the dock.

  4. Step 4

    Add the town

    Right-click the Sources list and choose Sarxina Source, then ChatSaga.

    Your own messages don’t make you an NPC, since you’re the hero. To try it alone, open the ChatSaga source’s Properties and tick Mercenary mode, or chat from a second account.

    No one showing up? Open http://127.0.0.1:4790/ in your browser. If your chatters are there, update ChatSaga in the manager’s dock. If the ⚙️ next to ChatSaga says the town can’t open on port 4790, something else is using that port.

  5. Step 5

    Walk around

    Right-click the ChatSaga source and choose Interact. Walk around with the arrow keys, and press Enter to talk to whoever you’re facing.

    Alternatively, play in the browser at http://127.0.0.1:4790/

Playing

Moving

Walk around with the arrow keys.

Press Enter, Space or Z to talk to whoever you’re facing.

Press Esc to open the menu.

In OBS, do all of this through Interact. In the browser, click the page first.

Your party

Talk to a chatter and choose Recruit. Up to four chatters can follow you, each with their class’s starting weapon. Lurkers can join too.

Anyone out of the stream for two hours leaves the party. To send someone home yourself, open the menu and choose Dismiss. Choose Status to see each member’s level, HP and stats.

Chat commands

Your chatters type these in Twitch chat.

TypeTo
!chatsaga status See your status panel on stream for a few seconds. Nothing is posted in chat.
!chatsaga changeclass <class> Switch to that class, for example !chatsaga changeclass knight

Classes: Swordsman, Sorcerer, Priest, Knight, Martial Artist, Magic Swordsman, Hunter, Bandit.
