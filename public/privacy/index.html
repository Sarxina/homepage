Privacy Policy

Sarxina Stats · Last updated October 2, 2026

Sarxina Stats is a private tool run by Sarxina, a VTuber, for Sarxina's own social media accounts. Sarxina is its only user. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it reads

  • The profile, posts and statistics of Sarxina's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube and Twitch.
  • Public counts on those posts, such as views, likes, shares and follower totals.
  • Comments left on Sarxina's own posts, including the commenter's public username and the comment text.

YouTube

For YouTube, the tool uses YouTube API Services. Its user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What it reads from YouTube: the video list of Sarxina's own channel with titles, publish dates and view, like and comment counts, and YouTube Analytics figures such as watch time and retention for those videos.
  • What it sends to YouTube: Sarxina's own videos, with their titles and descriptions, for upload to that channel.
  • YouTube statistics are stored only on Sarxina's own computer and private workspace. They are refreshed from YouTube at least every 30 days or deleted, and are not shared with anyone.
  • The tool's access to the channel can be revoked at any time on Google's security settings page. When access is revoked, or on request through the contact below, the stored YouTube data is deleted.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data from other people's accounts.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

Where the data is kept

Statistics are kept on Sarxina's own computer and in a private workspace that only Sarxina can open. Access tokens are kept on that computer and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's own settings.

Removal

If you commented on one of Sarxina's posts and want that comment removed from these private records, ask through the contact below. Deleting the comment on the platform also stops it from being read again.

Contact

Questions can be sent to @SarxinaVT on X.
